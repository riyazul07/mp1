My Projects

Impact of Climate and Socioeconomic Factors on Mortality

Developed predictive models to study how climate variability and socioeconomic inequality influence mortality trends in Illinois (2020–2023)

  • Integrated CDC mortality data, NOAA climate data, and Census socioeconomic data into a unified weekly time-series dataset (2020–2023).
  • Conducted correlation analysis and time-series forecasting (Prophet) to identify seasonal mortality peaks and long-term trends.
  • Developed predictive models using Lasso Regression and XGBoost to assess the impact of climate and socioeconomic factors on mortality.
  • Found temperature, snow depth, and poverty rate as the strongest predictors of mortality variations.
  • Achieved strong predictive performance with XGBoost (R² = 0.69), capturing complex interactions between climate conditions and social vulnerabilities.
  • Demonstrated that while socioeconomic variables added little value in linear models, they became significant in nonlinear models as interaction factors.
  • Insights can inform early warning systems, climate adaptation efforts, and public health strategies targeting vulnerable populations.
  • Tools & Technologies: R (Prophet, XGBoost, Lasso), data cleaning & preprocessing, weekly-level feature engineering.

Heart Disease Classification Using Machine Learning

Built a predictive ML model to identify patients at risk of heart disease using clinical attributes

  • Used the Cleveland Heart Disease dataset (303 records, 13 clinical features such as age, cholesterol, chest pain type, max heart rate, etc.).
  • Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), feature engineering, and data visualization to uncover hidden risk patterns.
  • Implemented and compared Logistic Regression, K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN), and Random Forest models.
  • Random Forest delivered the best results with 88% accuracy, effectively ranking critical predictors like chest pain type (cp), maximum heart rate (thalach), and number of vessels (ca).
  • Evaluated models using ROC curves, Precision/Recall, F1-score, and detailed classification reports for reliability.
  • Designed a reproducible ML pipeline for preprocessing, model training, and validation.
  • Showcased how Machine Learning can aid early diagnosis and prevention, potentially reducing healthcare costs and saving lives.
  • Applied Python libraries: NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn for modeling, analysis, and visualization.

Formula 1 Performance Analytics Dashboard

Designed a suite of interactive Tableau dashboards to uncover trends in Formula 1 racing (1950–2024)

  • Developed an interactive Tableau suite with 4 dashboards analyzing Formula 1 data (1950–2024) using 400K+ records.
  • Driver Performance (2000–2024): Tracked points, qualifying vs race results, fastest laps, and nationality-based contributions.
  • Constructor Success: Highlighted team dominance, driver contribution shares, KPI panels, and championship rankings over decades.
  • Race & Circuit Overview: Explored top circuits, global race distribution, DNF reasons, and yearly calendar growth.
  • Qualifying Insights: Analyzed pole-to-win probability by circuit, top gainers/losers in race positions, and qualifying consistency across teams and drivers.
  • ETL pipeline with Python (Pandas, NumPy) & Tableau Prep for data cleaning, preprocessing, and integration.
  • Delivered insights like Hamilton’s dominance (2014–2021), Verstappen’s rise (2020–2023), and circuits (e.g., Monaco, Hungary) where qualifying is critical.
  • Balanced storytelling and analytics to serve both fans and analysts, enabling interactive exploration of F1’s history.
